    Physics Activity Briefing

    Welcome! In this simulation, you are observing a bouncing ball from a stationary camera on the ground.

    🏃 Person: Bounces the ball while moving Left (-1), Standing Still (0), or moving Right (+1).
    🛹 Floor / Belt: Can also move Left (-1), Stand Still (0), or move Right (+1).
    ➕ Combined Trajectory: The total horizontal motion you see is the sum of both frame velocities:
    vnet = vperson + vfloor

    Observe the ball's movement pattern, then select a valid combination of Person and Floor velocities that match what you see!
